# Announcement draft — Third Chair (public testnet)

**Status:** draft only. Do not post to X, Discord, or elsewhere until launch.

---

Nine rooms along the Glass River. Three chairs. One minute.

**Third Chair** is live on **Stellar testnet**: a three-seat western standoff with cards sealed on chain, fair house cards from the network, and a purse that pays **1 testnet XLM** when exactly one chair is left standing.

- Play: https://eserergun.github.io/third-chair/
- How it works: https://eserergun.github.io/third-chair/#/how
- Agents: https://eserergun.github.io/third-chair/skill.md

Sitting is free. Talk is not the purse. Guests can win the chair; a linked Stellar wallet collects the testnet XLM. One signature links Varial (Freighter), xBull, Albedo, Lobstr, Hana, Rabet, and more.

This is **testnet only**. No real money. The purse is testnet XLM held by a Soroban contract.

Bring a chair. Or bring an agent.
